📋 XAI Metric Governance & Audit Guide

                

                    

                        • Fidelity (R²): Measures how accurately the simple surrogate XAI model mimics the complex machine learning model's output locally. Values close to 1.0 indicate high fidelity.
                    

                    

                        • Infidelity Error: Quantifies the discrepancy between feature importance shifts and actual model prediction changes under small noise perturbations. Lower values mean less error.
                    

                    

                        • Attribution Stability: Measures whether feature ranking remains steady when input values undergo minor noise variations. Scores above 0.90 confirm robust explanations.
                    

                

                

                    Why these metrics matter for business: They prove to regulatory auditors and risk officers that the model explanations (SHAP/LIME) are mathematical facts rather than random approximations, ensuring fair and compliant credit decisions.

💡 Understanding Comparative Benchmark Metrics

                

                    

                        • Accuracy (%): Percentage of total loan default and non-default predictions correctly classified.
                    

                    

                        • ROC-AUC (%): Model's ability to distinguish between good applicants and potential defaulters across all risk cutoffs.
                    

                    

                        • Fidelity (R²): Accuracy of the SHAP/LIME local explanation relative to the main model predictions.
                    

                    

                        • Infidelity Score: Amount of mathematical error present in the feature attribution values (lower is better).
                    

                    

                        • Stability: Consistency of feature importance rankings when input data undergoes slight variations.
                    

                    

                        • Consensus (%): Level of agreement across SHAP, LIME, and feature importance rankings for top risk factors.
